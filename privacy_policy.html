Aayushkalpa Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Aayushkalpa is dedicated to fostering social welfare, community volunteering (seva), environmental care, and student value education. We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of our volunteers, students, and users. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, safeguard, and manage your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly through our mobile application:

  • Account Details: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and assigned user role.
  • Volunteer Identity Verification: Profile photo and government identity document (Aadhaar card) submitted exclusively for verifying Lead and General Volunteer applicants.
  • Event Registration Data: Event participation, availability, preferred seva categories, skills, and emergency contact details.
  • Student & Learning Records: Enrolled workshop modules, attendance, quiz submissions, and direct feedback.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is used solely for non-profit and community coordination purposes:

  • To verify volunteer credentials and authorize role approvals.
  • To schedule and organize seva events, coordinate venue logistics, and manage volunteer capacities.
  • To send transactional push notifications, event reminders, and emergency broadcasts.
  • To manage classes, deliver educational materials, and issue completion certificates.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data and uploaded verification documents are securely stored and encrypted in Google Cloud and Firebase infrastructure. We employ industry-standard SSL encryption in transit, secure cloud storage buckets with role-based access rules, and automated session management to safeguard your information.

5. Third-Party Services & Device Permissions

Our app integrates with standard Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: For secure authentication and encrypted data persistence.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): For delivering timely volunteer notifications and event alerts.

Device Permissions:

  • Camera / Photo Storage: Optional access granted only when uploading verification documents or profile pictures.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): To receive event reminder alerts.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have full ownership and rights over your personal data:

  • You may review, update, or correct your personal profile at any time within the application.
  • You can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal records at any time via the in-app Delete Account feature in your Profile settings, or by emailing kalpaaayush@gmail.com.
