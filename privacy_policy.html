1. Introduction

Welcome to Aayush Kalpa (referenced as "Aayush Kalpa", "we", "us", or "our"). Aayush Kalpa is a dual-portal application composed of a Volunteer Module (for administrators, lead volunteers, and volunteers managing outreach and events) and a Learning Module (for students accessing classes and educational materials).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and protect your information when you register and interact with our services via the mobile application (distributed on the Google Play Store) or the web portal. By accessing or using Aayush Kalpa, you consent to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized and functional experience across both portals, we collect different types of personal information depending on your role within the application.

A. Student Information (Learning Module)

During student signup and profile management, we collect:

  • Full Name: To establish identity, customize communication, and generate course completion certificates.
  • Email Address: Restricted exclusively to @gmail.com domains. This serves as the unique login credential and receiving address for application approval updates.
  • Phone Number: For emergency contacts, account retrieval, and communication.
  • Age & Gender: To understand demographics and tailor educational coursework.
  • Highest Qualification & Occupation: To customize learning material difficulty and target relevant courses.
  • Location & Course Interests: City and State of residence to customize outreach efforts, and selected topics of interest (e.g., Mathematics, Computer Science) to recommend relevant classes.
  • Password: Collected during registration and processed securely by Firebase Authentication. We never store passwords in plaintext.
  • Student ID: A sequential ID (e.g., FG1001) automatically generated upon admin approval.

B. Volunteer Information (Volunteer Module)

During the general volunteer registration request, we collect:

  • Personal & Physical Details: First name, last name, date of birth, gender, physical address, geographical location, profile photo (for identity verification), and blood group (for safety and emergency coordination).
  • Contact Details: Primary email (restricted to @gmail.com), secondary email, primary contact number, and secondary contact number.
  • Identity Verification:
    • Aadhaar Number: Users input their Aadhaar number. The raw number text is processed server-side to save only a cryptographic SHA-256 hash (aadhaarHash) and the last 4 digits (aadhaarLast4) in Firestore for deduplication.
    • Aadhaar Card Document: To prevent fraudulent requests, we collect a photo/scan of your Aadhaar card. This image is stored securely within our private Firebase Storage bucket (accessible only to system administrators) for verification. It is restricted from public access and deleted once verification is completed.
  • Professional Details: Associated organization name, organization contact number, and your designated role (e.g., Volunteer, Lead Volunteer).

C. Usage & Interactive Data

As you interact with Aayush Kalpa, we generate and collect:

  • Class Progress Data: Tracking enrolled courses, course status (Registered, In Progress, Completed), and certificate tracking details.
  • Activity Streaks: Automated records tracking lastActivityDate and consecutive daily active streaks (currentStreak) to encourage active learning.
  • Chat Messages: Messages, timestamps, and attachment metadata exchanged in public group chats (e.g., Students Chat) or direct chats between volunteers and students.
  • Feedback Queries: Content submitted via the feedback forms, along with your name, email, and user ID.

3. Device Permissions

Depending on your actions, our mobile application may request the following device permissions. You can control these permissions at any time through your device settings:

  • Microphone Permission Used in Chat: Used strictly when you long-press the microphone icon in the Chat Details screen to record and send audio voice messages to other users. Recorded audio files are stored securely in Firebase Storage.
  • Camera & Gallery Access Optional: Used to take a profile picture or select files/images from your device gallery to share as attachments within the chat section.
  • Storage & Files Access Optional: Required to download learning materials, upload files via the file picker, and temporarily store recorded voice files before secure transmission.
  • Network & Internet access Required: Vital to synchronize data with Firebase Services, load course lists, process video playback, and run live notifications.

4. How We Use Your Information

Aayush Kalpa utilizes the collected information for the following administrative, security, and educational purposes:

  1. Portal Access & Authentication: To create, manage, and verify user profiles and distinguish permissions between Students, Volunteers, Lead Volunteers, and Superadmins.
  2. Identity Verification: Processing SHA-256 hashed Aadhaar details to verify volunteer authenticity and prevent duplicate registrations.
  3. Educational Management: Managing class enrollments, tracking progress stats, validating completions, and maintaining learning activity streaks.
  4. Interactive Communications: Supporting real-time group and direct chat messaging, including text, image sharing, and voice messages.
  5. Email Notifications: Utilizing automated cloud-mailer operations to notify students of registration approvals or rejections, and managing password reset requests.
  6. Service Enhancement: Analyzing feedback submissions to troubleshoot bugs, adjust feature layouts, and update FAQs.

5. Data Storage, Security & Sharing

We prioritize data security and enforce modern standards to protect your personal details from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

A. Infrastructure & Storage

All personal profiles, chat logs, files, and registration details are securely hosted on Google Firebase Infrastructure. The app utilizes:

  • Firebase Authentication: Encrypts and processes login passwords securely.
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores structured text data (profiles, settings, chats) in location asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) to ensure low latency and localized compliance.
  • Firebase Storage: Handles media attachments (chat pictures and recorded audio files).

B. Third-Party Integrations

We work with trusted external services to power specific application actions:

  • YouTube API Services: Our learning module streams educational material using YouTube players. By viewing these videos, you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Zoom Meetings: Our online virtual classrooms redirect users to Zoom live sessions. Participating in Zoom events is governed by Zoom's Privacy Statement.

C. Data Sharing Policy

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or commercial entities. Data access is restricted to:

  • System administrators (Superadmins) who manage student approvals and volunteer assignments.
  • Required automated transmissions to Firebase backend systems.
  • Legal disclosure if required by regulatory authorities under applicable laws.

6. Data Deletion & Your Rights

We believe you should have complete control over your personal information. In accordance with Google Play Store developer policies, we offer clear options to access, modify, or erase your data:

A. Direct In-App Account Deletion

You can permanently erase your account details directly inside the application. When you trigger account deletion:

  • The system cleans up your Cloud Firestore profile document.
  • All associated course enrollment records are deleted.
  • Your Firebase Authentication credentials are deleted from our servers.
  • This deletion is immediate, permanent, and cannot be undone.

B. Interactive Data Deletion Request

If you are unable to access the app or want to request a manual deletion of your volunteer requests, student logs, or profile details, you can generate a deletion request email using the tool below. We will process all manual requests within 7 business days.

Open Mail Client

7. Children's Privacy

Aayush Kalpa provides educational learning content which may be accessed by children under the age of 18 (e.g., student learners). We take extra precautions to protect underage users:

  • We do not display commercial advertising to any students.
  • We recommend that underage students register and navigate the app with the guidance and consent of a parent or guardian.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that their child has provided personal information without permission, they can contact us at kalpaaayush@gmail.com to request immediate deletion of that data.
